002 — In development
SYNAPSE (the hard one)

"You don't lack willpower. You have a hijacked reward system. This fixes it."

Dopamine addiction isn't a character flaw — it's an architecture problem. SYNAPSE maps your specific compulsion loop, then deploys a personalized AI protocol built to match how your particular loop broke.

A five-step confession flow extracts the pattern; streak logic, relapse response, and milestone drops keep it evolving. Every request runs through a server that verifies who you actually are — no client can fake its way in.

The hardest feature to build was the one that had to feel human.
