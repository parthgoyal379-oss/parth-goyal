001 — Featured project
ClassPredictor
"Students don't fail because Class 11 is hard.
They fail because hidden foundation gaps compound silently."
Most academic tools tell you what score you got. This one tells you where you're heading — before it's too late to change it.

The insight was simple: prediction beats motivation. If a student can see the trajectory of their class division in real time, they stop guessing and start acting.

Built under real exam pressure. Tested by real students. Shipped alone.
